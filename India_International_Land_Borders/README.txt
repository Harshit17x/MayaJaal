India International Land Borders — processed from Survey of India OUTLINE_OF_INDIA

CRS: WGS 84 (EPSG:4326)

Included neighbours:
Pakistan, China, Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, Myanmar

The user-supplied Survey of India dataset is a polygon outline. This package contains a LINE layer extracted from that outline,
with coastline portions (Arabian Sea, Bay of Bengal, etc.) intentionally omitted.

Because the Survey of India OUTLINE_OF_INDIA polygon does not carry an attribute identifying each outline segment by neighbouring
country, the extraction is a GIS-derived working layer using border-location corridors. The geometry therefore follows the supplied
Survey of India outline, but this is not itself a separately published Survey of India international-boundary line product.

Disputed/claimed areas are not adjudicated here.
